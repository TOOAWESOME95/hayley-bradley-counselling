Areas of Support

How I Can Help

Every person who comes to counselling brings something different. Below are some of the areas I support most often - but if what you're experiencing isn't listed, please don't let that stop you from reaching out.

Bereavement

Losing someone brings grief that doesn't move in a straight line. I offer a gentle, unhurried space to talk about your loss, your memories, and the person you've lost - with no expectation to "move on" before you're ready.

Addiction & Addictive Behaviours

Whether it's substances, alcohol, or other addictive patterns, I offer a compassionate, judgement-free space to explore what's underneath the behaviour and support you in finding a way forward that feels right for you.

Anxiety & Stress

When worry or overwhelm start to take over daily life, counselling can offer a calmer perspective. Together we'll explore what's driving the anxiety and build gentle, grounding tools to help you feel more at ease.

Life Challenges

Redundancy, relationship breakdown, illness, big transitions - life doesn't pause for us to catch up. I provide steady, supportive space to process change and reconnect with your own resilience.

Men's Mental Health

Many men find it hard to find the space - or the words - to talk about how they're really doing. I offer a warm, non-judgemental environment where it's safe to open up, without pressure or expectation.

Feeling Stuck

Sometimes there isn't one big issue - just a sense of drifting, or being unable to move forward. Counselling can help you get curious about what's holding you back and find clarity on your next step.

What to Expect

Sessions, at a glance

50-minute sessions

Weekly, fortnightly, or as often as feels right - one-to-one, in a calm, confidential setting.

Online or in person

Sessions available via secure video call, or face-to-face - whichever feels most comfortable.

No pressure, no labels

A person-centred approach means you set the pace. There's no agenda beyond your own.

A space to be heard

Not sure where to start? That's okay.

Get in touch and we can have a short, no-obligation conversation about what you're looking for and whether counselling with me feels like the right fit.
