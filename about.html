My Story

I've always found that the outdoors has a way of putting things into perspective - the rhythm of the tide, the quiet of a woodland walk, the space to simply breathe. That connection to nature sits at the heart of how I work as a counsellor, and it's something I try to bring into every session, whether we're talking indoors or walking along the coast.

I hold a BA (Hons) and I'm a Registered Member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (MBACP), working within their Ethical Framework. My approach is person-centred: I believe you are the expert in your own life, and my role is to walk alongside you with warmth, empathy and complete acceptance - not to direct or diagnose, but to help you find your own way through.

"I'm very holistic in how I work - mind, body and environment all play a part in how we heal."

My Approach

Person-centred counselling is built on three core conditions: unconditional positive regard, empathy, and congruence (genuineness). In practice, that means you'll never feel judged, rushed, or like you need to perform being "okay."

Although my approach is predominantly person-centred, everybody is treated as an individual - so I also work pluralistically, drawing on additional modalities such as Compassion Focused Therapy and mindfulness-based techniques where they're helpful. These are adapted to work with you specifically, to find the best possible outcome for you as a client, gently encouraging self-awareness and a mindful connection to living in the present.

Person-Centred Pluralistic Compassion Focused Therapy Mindfulness-Based
  • Unconditional positive regard - you're accepted here, without conditions.
  • Deep empathy - I listen to understand your world as you experience it.
  • Pluralistic & tailored - Compassion Focused Therapy and mindfulness-based techniques, adapted to you.
  • Genuine & congruent - real, honest presence, session after session.
